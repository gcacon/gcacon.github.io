Why you should save your old hardware

Does your operating system work for you or against you? Like most people, you probably want to turn on your machine and use it without asking questions. But easy, question-free computer use is becoming more and more of a costly luxury with each passing year.

In October of 2025, Microsoft ended Windows 10 support and stranded hundreds of millions of devices that otherwise have perfectly-still-functioning electronics but are technically incompatible with Windows 11. But this does NOT mean your otherwise-fine computer still running Windows 10 is all of a sudden unusable junk. More and more people are waking up to the fact that modern versions of the Windows operating system (like Windows 11) use quite a lot of RAM and CPU power for what just a fancy interface with some additional features that you never asked for and will probably never use. That is exactly the reason why older computers and laptops just don't run it well anymore.

What AI is and isn’t: Sorting out the Wheat from the Chaff

So-called "AI" is not intelligent in any sentient sense, as the word intelligence might suggest. First and foremost, the usage of the AI label is all about marketing and hype-mongering. The actual technology that so many people these days are calling AI is the Large Language Model (LLM). So what actually is this technology? We can think of a computer as a glorified calculator. And by that, we can say that an LLM is just a really high-powered computer application. The LLMs you’ve used and interacted with run on computers with magnitudes more processing power (and corresponding resource-consumption!) than a typical consumer laptop or desktop PC.

We've seen AI proponents make all sorts of wild claims about what this tech can do. It's supposedly going to replace all menial work within a few years. Everyone is going to have their own virtual assistant doing all those repetitive and mind-numbing tasks for them. Some are even claiming that The most zealous of AI proponents claim that this technology will soon achieve AGI (Artificial General Intelligence) and the world as we know it will never be the same. And some think this hypothetical AGI will go rogue and decide one day that we puny mortals no longer need to exist. Crazy, huh?

Now let's step back for a moment and measure up what LLMs are and what LLMs can do versus what all the hype says. LLMs are fundamentally statistical pattern-matching systems trained to predict the next word in a sequence, rather than entities possessing genuine understanding, logical reasoning, or causal awareness of the physical world. While they excel at generating human-like text, they do not "know" facts in the human sense and lack an internal model of reality, meaning their outputs are based on surface semantics and probabilistic correlations rather than true comprehension. In a nutshell, we could summarize the capabilities of an LLM as “super advanced guessing.”
